game 02

devilish calc

Equations flash one at a time. Solve each one mentally — but enter the answer from N steps ago.

How to play

  1. An equation appears. Solve it in your head but don't type yet.
  2. When the next equation appears, type the answer to the previous one.
  3. At N=1 you answer one behind. At N=2, two behind — much harder.
  4. 5 correct answers in a row increases N by 1.
  5. You have 3 lives. A wrong answer costs a life and shows the correct answer.
