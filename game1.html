game 01

number flash

Numbers appear briefly. Memorize where each one is, then click the target positions in order.

How to play

  1. Start with 3 numbered circles.
  2. When the circles disappear, click the blank targets in order: 1, 2, 3.
  3. Each successful round adds another circle and shortens the flash.
  4. A wrong click shows the answer and lets you try the next attempt.
