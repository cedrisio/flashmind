how to play

  1. Memorize — numbered circles appear briefly. Track their positions.
  2. Recall — circles vanish. Click the zones in order: 1 → 2 → 3…
  3. Progress — each round adds one circle and shortens the flash time.
  4. Survive — one wrong click ends the run. No upper limit on circles.
